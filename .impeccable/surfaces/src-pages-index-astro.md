## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a premium trading-card set, "The Rookie Set": every project is a collectible card with the work on the front and real stats on the back; flagships are holo parallels. It refuses the dark-name-plus-thumbnail-grid designer portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Grading-case black ground (#0c0d12) under card-stock bone (#e8e4da). Chrome refractor foil (iridescent conic sheen tracking the pointer) only on the three flagship holos. Red #ff3b1f marks flagship rarity only; cobalt #2b4bff marks the primary action only. Big Shoulders Display for nameplates and numerals, Schibsted Grotesk for prose. Cards carry print bleed, corner radius 14px, serial numbers like "01/03", set code "IM-26". The binder checklist page is the gallery.

STORY: The visitor rips the pack, sees three famous-name holos, flips one to read real stats, browses the binder of every card by set (Carousels, Brand & Print, Motion, Ads & AI), reads the "how a set gets made" process (sprint into membership), the FAQ, and books a call from the wrapper's tear strip or the closing card.

Right 60%: three holo cards fanned at hero scale (Steven Bartlett, Metro Media House, Vryks), tilting with the pointer; the center one is pulled forward.
