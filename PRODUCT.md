## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output) with a content-collection folder per project, deployed free to GitHub Pages via GitHub Actions at ishan.vryks.com (CNAME on Hostinger DNS). Zero recurring cost is a hard constraint.

## Users

Primary: founders building a personal or company brand (CEOs, VCs, operators) who are deciding whether to hire a senior creative to own their brand and content. They arrive from LinkedIn, a referral or a DM, often on a phone, and judge within seconds by whether the work looks like what they want for themselves. Secondary visitors (creators, podcast teams, startups, agencies) evaluate the same proof.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Ishan Mitra, Brand & Content Director and founder of Vryks Media. It exists to convert high-ticket founders ($5k–15k first engagement) into a booked intro call. Success = qualified Cal.com bookings.

## Positioning

One person who designs the brand and then runs the content that grows it: identity, carousels, video, repurposing, creative direction, websites, social management and CRM setup, backed by the Vryks team for bigger scopes. Proof-led: 40M+ views from Instagram carousels alone, for Steven Bartlett, Daily Mail (dmg newmedia), Metro Media House and other founder and creator brands.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the argument: every screen earns trust through real artifacts before any claim.
2. Proof over adjectives: numbers and recognisable names, never invented social proof.
3. One clear next step: every path ends at booking a call.
4. Premium means restraint in the ask and confidence in the craft.
5. Adding new work must stay a drop-in task.
